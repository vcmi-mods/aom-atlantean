# Age of Mythology: Atlantis (VCMI town mod)

**神话时代：大西洲** — the Atlanteans of *Age of Mythology: Retold* turned into the town of Atlantis for [VCMI](https://vcmi.eu).

## Contents

- 43 creatures: seven town levels, each with a base form and two alternative upgrades that can be converted into each other in town, plus out-of-town units with their own map dwellings
- 3 hero classes, 25 heroes (the major gods Kronos, Oranos and Gaia lead the three classes), Chief Oracle commander
- 2 racial secondary skills: Triune Order and Divine Echo, built on the "Titan Aspects" system (every creature form carries the aspect of Oranos, Kronos or Gaia)
- 12 god-power spells, plus Return to Atlantis, learned at the Grail
- Town: building tree with the Ages of the original game, dwellings, mage guild, animated town screen, hall, map look, siege screen, puzzle map, tavern video, music and boat
- Artifacts: the Titan Legacy set (7 components and 1 combined artifact)
- Tavern rumors, the Cheiroballista war machine, the Tartarean Rift creature bank

Submods (both optional, enabled by default):

- **Coastal Land Terrain** (海滩地形): the Coastal Land terrain with its map decorations, mines, battlefield and music, made the native terrain of Atlantis. Terrain originally by **Zeryss** (Coastal Lands); adapted by yche2990 (lighter ground tiles, Age of Mythology: Retold soundtrack as terrain music, random map biomes, no HotA dependency).
- **Atlantis Wiki Page** (大西洲百科): adds a glossary page to the in-game wiki explaining the Titan Aspects, Favor, god powers, Ages and Return to Atlantis.

Languages: English (base) and Chinese. Players using any other game language get English.

Sprites are included at 1x only; VCMI scales them up for higher interface scaling.

## Requirements

VCMI 1.8 or later (the mod uses Lua battle scripts). No other mods are required.
